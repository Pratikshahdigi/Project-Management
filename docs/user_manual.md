# User Manual: Agency Operating System (Agency OS)

Welcome to the Agency OS User Manual. This guide provides onboarding steps for team members, managers, and designers to leverage client CRM tools, calendar post scheduling, vaults storage, and local AI.

---

## 1. Navigating the CEO Command Center
The Dashboard tab acts as the primary hub:
- **Financial Analytics:** Displays Monthly Revenue, ARR projections, and active accounts limit logs.
- **AI Balanced Assignment Tool:** Calculates workload capacity maps and suggests optimal team members for incoming tasks.
- **Risks & Signals:** Warns you if a client renewal drops below 60% based on recent response metrics.

---

## 2. Using the Client CRM
The CRM records contract parameters, ad budgets, and social metrics.
- Go to **CRM Clients**.
- Click **Register Client** to append new records.
- To inspect files and folders linked to a client, select **Client Vault**.
- View calculated engagement and risk logs in real-time.

---

## 3. Creating & Approving Tasks
- Add tickets inside **Tasks & Kanban**.
- Transition tickets across boards (Todo → In Progress → In Review → Completed).
- Designers submit visual assets directly to **In Review** cards.
- Team Leads and Managers click **Approve Design** to progress the state.

---

## 4. Scheduling Social Campaigns
The social media tool publishes content directly to Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn.
- Navigate to **Social Planner**.
- Choose target client and compose text body.
- **AI Pre-Publishing Score:** Review calculated engagement rating prior to scheduling.
- Click **Route Approvals** to submit the draft.

---

## 5. Storing Files in Private Vaults
Self-hosted MinIO object vault handles all assets.
- Navigate to **Storage Vaults**.
- Directories (`Design`, `Video`, `Document`, `Report`, `Contract`) are auto-created when clients register.
- Select a directory and click **Upload File** to drop file payloads locally.
